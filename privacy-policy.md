# video-poster-automatic Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

video-poster-automatic ("the app") is a private tool operated by a single individual (the "operator") to produce and
publish videos on the operator's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what Google user data the app
accesses and how it is handled.

## Who can use the app

Only the operator signs in to the app, with the Google accounts that own the operator's YouTube channels and
Google Drive. The app has no public users and does not collect data about anyone else.

## Google data the app accesses

When the operator signs in with Google, the app may request:

- **YouTube (upload and manage):** to upload videos to the operator's channels, set their title, description,
  tags, privacy, publish schedule and content labels, and edit those videos later.
- **YouTube (read-only):** to read the channels' basic information and their videos' status, so it can confirm
  the right channel is connected and avoid scheduling conflicts.
- **YouTube Analytics (read-only):** to read view, watch-time and subscriber statistics of the channels' own
  videos, used to plan future videos.
- **Google Drive (files created by the app only):** to store and retrieve the app's own video files, images and
  project data. The app cannot see any other files in the operator's Drive.

## How the data is used and stored

- The data is used only to operate the operator's own channels, as described above.
- Sign-in tokens, analytics figures and project files are stored on the operator's private server and, for
  files the app creates, in the operator's Google Drive.
- The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
- The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
  [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
  including the Limited Use requirements.

## YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and Google's handling of data is described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the
[Google Account permissions page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). After revocation, the app can no
longer access the account; stored tokens are deleted from the operator's server, and the operator can delete
the app's files from Google Drive at any time.
