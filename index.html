video-poster-automatic

video-poster-automatic is a private, single-operator tool that produces and publishes videos to its owner's own YouTube channels (Bankai Basics and The Third Variable). It uses Google sign-in only to upload and schedule those channels' videos, read their YouTube Analytics, and store the project's own files in its owner's Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.
